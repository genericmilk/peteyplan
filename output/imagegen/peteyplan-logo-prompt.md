# PeteyPlan logo

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The original alpha is preserved in `peteyplan-mark-source.png`; the web asset is resized to 256 pixels. The app and favicon tint that same silhouette for each theme.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: PeteyPlan app header mark and tiny browser favicon.
Primary request: one simple, distinctive geometric P monogram with a subtle checkmark-shaped negative space suggesting planning and completion. Compact bold silhouette, instantly readable at 16 pixels.
Style/medium: extremely clean flat vector-like graphic, solid monochrome black ink, crisp smooth edges, no outlines.
Composition/framing: one single mark centered on a square, occupying 88% of the canvas height and width with a small even clear margin. Transparent background, including all negative spaces.
Text: no wordmark, no captions.
Constraints: one coherent symbol, thick sturdy strokes, minimal internal detail, truly transparent alpha background. No tile or enclosing square, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no 3D, no mockup, no watermark. The alpha silhouette will be tinted blue in Day mode and pale periwinkle in Night mode by the app.
